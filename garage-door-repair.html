Common Repair Diagnostics We Handle

Our trucks arrive fully stocked with heavy-duty springs, replacement cables, gears, rollers, and sensors, finishing most jobs on the first trip.

Torsion & Extension Spring Repair

Springs balance the heavy weight of the door panels. A snapped spring prevents automated or manual operation. We replace them with high-cycle springs designed for 15,000+ operations.

Off-Track Re-Alignment

If a cable jumps a drum or rollers wear out, your door can jam at an angle. Do not try to force it down! We secure the door structure, align the tracks, and thread new cables safely.

Opener Motor Repair & Reset

If your opener hums but doesn't move, or safety eyes are blinking, it could indicate stripped internal nylon gears, board faults, or sensor misalignment. We service all brands.

Cable, Roller, and Pulley Swap

Frayed tension cables or rusted ball-bearing rollers cause noisy operations and strain the opener. We replace rusted hardware with heavy-gauge galvanized steel equivalents.

Safety Advisory

Why DIY Garage Repair is Highly Risky

Before grabbing winding bars or structural clamps to service your garage door components, be aware of the critical safety hazards associated with high-tension garage parts.

  • Torsion Shaft Force: Springs hold up to 300+ lbs of structural tension. Unwinding them without professional winding cones can shatter bones instantly.
  • Fingers & Cable Shear: Frayed steel cables under tension behave like steel saws if they snap during manual tightening.
  • Crush Hazards: An unbalanced door panel assembly can drop instantly from its track if structural roller balances are altered.
