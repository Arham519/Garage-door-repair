Our Process

4-Step Installation Workflow

  • 1
    Onsite Measurements & Design: We inspect your structural headers and select the appropriate dimensions, spring balance weights, and styles.
  • 2
    Old Door Removal & Disposal: We carefully disconnect high-tension assemblies, dismantle old panels, and haul them away.
  • 3
    Track & Panel Assembly: We level vertical tracks, install new weather stripping, assemble the panels, and install torsion shafts.
  • 4
    Opener Integration & Balance Testing: We calibrate opener force limits, program Wi-Fi features, and confirm smooth balance operations.
